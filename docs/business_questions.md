# Business Context and Problem Statements

## Business Context

Olist is a Brazilian e-commerce marketplace that connects small sellers with larger online storefronts and supports the logistics involved in fulfilling customer orders.

For this analysis, the business priorities are growth, delivery reliability, seller performance, and customer retention.

## PS-1: Revenue and Demand

Where does revenue come from, how is it trending, and which product categories, customer states, and payment behaviors drive it?

This analysis will examine:
- Revenue and order trends over time
- Revenue by product category
- Revenue by customer state
- Average order value
- Payment method and installment behavior

## PS-2: Delivery Performance

How fast and reliably are orders delivered, how often are orders late compared with their estimated delivery dates, and does late delivery affect customer reviews?

This analysis will examine:
- Average delivery time
- On-time versus late delivery rates
- Delivery performance by geography
- Relationship between delivery delays and review scores

## PS-3: Seller Quality

Which sellers perform best and worst based on revenue, ratings, delivery performance, and cancellations, and how concentrated is marketplace GMV among sellers?

This analysis will examine:
- Revenue by seller
- Seller review ratings
- Seller delivery performance
- Seller cancellation rates
- GMV concentration among top sellers

## PS-4: Customer Retention

How many customers return to make additional purchases, how does retention change across customer cohorts, and which customers represent the highest-value segments?

This analysis will examine:
- Repeat customer rate
- Purchase frequency
- Customer cohorts
- Customer lifetime value
- RFM customer segmentation
